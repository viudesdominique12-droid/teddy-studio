How renting works

From your list to your set

  1. 01

    Choose your equipment

    Add items from the catalogue, or describe what your production needs.

  2. 02

    Send your dates and intended use

    The rental period, the location, and whether you need an operator.

  3. 03

    We check availability

    Nothing is booked until we have confirmed the equipment is free for your dates.

  4. 04

    You receive your quote

    The rate, the included accessories and the rental conditions.

  5. 05

    Pickup or delivery

    As agreed in your quote, then returned at the end of the rental.

Rental conditions

Clear terms, confirmed with your quote

These points are general. The exact conditions of your rental, including any deposit, are set out with your quote.

Rental conditionsSheet 05 / 05

  • 03A deposit or identification may be required, depending on the equipment.
  • 04Included accessories are listed with your quote.
  • 05Some equipment may require a trained operator or additional technical support.
  • 06Rental periods: half day, full day, weekend, weekly or long term, depending on the equipment.
