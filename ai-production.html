Transparency

How we label AI work

Not every project is entirely AI-generated. Each example we publish says how it was made, so you always know what you are looking at.

AI-assisted

Human-led production using AI tools

Our team directs, produces and finishes the work. AI tools support specific steps along the way.

AI-generated

Created primarily with generative tools

The images, video or sound come mainly from generative AI tools, guided and selected by our team.

Concept demonstration

An idea shown, not a commissioned piece

Made to show a style, a technique or a possibility. It is never presented as client work.
