Five stages, from your first conversation with our team to the support that follows the event.

  1. Cue 01

    Consultation and concept development

    We start from your occasion, your guests and your goals, and shape a concept around them.

    StandbyGo
  2. Cue 02

    Planning and budgeting

    Schedule, venue options, suppliers and a budget, set out and agreed with you.

    StandbyGo
  3. Cue 03

    Coordination and preparation

    StandbyGo
  4. Cue 04
