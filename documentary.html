What we put first

Real stories, told with care

  1. p. I

    Storytelling

    Thoughtful research first, then a clear story: who it is about, what is at stake, and why it deserves to be seen.

    PrincipleI
  2. p. II

    Authenticity

    Real people, real places and real voices, filmed as they are and heard in their own words.

    PrincipleII
  3. p. III

    Production quality

    Professional filming, location audio, editing, color grading and sound design, so the story holds up on every screen it reaches.

    PrincipleIII
  4. p. IV

    Responsible documentation

    Consent, accuracy and respect for the people and communities who share their stories with us.

    PrincipleIV
